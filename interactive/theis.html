Theis solution

s(r,t) = Q4πT W(u)
where u= r2S 4Tt
W(u)= ∫u∞ e−xxdx =−γ−lnu +u −u24 +u318 −u496 +⋯

Euler–Mascheroni constant: γ ≈ 0.5772156649

s: drawdown [m] Q: pumping rate [m³/d] T = Kb: transmissivity [m²/d] K: hydraulic conductivity [m/d] b: aquifer thickness [m] S: storativity [–] r: distance from well [m] t: time since pumping [d]

Key assumptions

  • Confined, homogeneous, isotropic aquifer with uniform thickness and infinite extent.
  • Initially uniform hydraulic head; constant-rate pumping begins instantaneously.
  • Fully penetrating line well; horizontal radial Darcy flow.
  • Negligible wellbore storage, skin and well losses.
  • Instantaneous storage response; constant transmissivity and storativity.
